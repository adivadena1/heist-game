**Aurum Heist** — a top-down heist game in three floors of the Meridian Bank. Sneak past patrolling guards and their vision cones, slip under sweeping security cameras, time the pulsing laser grids, and lift every piece of loot — gold bars, cash stacks, diamonds, and the crown diamond itself — before the alarm meter fills and the bank locks down. Tools of the trade: smoke pellets to vanish, an EMP burst to black out cameras and lasers, and a lockpick for the vault doors (the Aurum Vault itself needs a stolen keycard). Grab the loot, mind the alarm, and get to the getaway van.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network requests — plain static files under `game/`. Keyboard + mouse: WASD/arrows to move, Shift to sneak, click to throw smoke, hold E to pick a lock, Q for an EMP burst, R to retry, P to pause, M to mute.
